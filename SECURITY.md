# Security

## Vulnerabilities in BeforeRun

Report vulnerabilities in this skill or repository to the maintainers privately if an appropriate private channel is available. No private reporting channel is configured or promised by this document. Do not publish exploit details or sensitive information in a public issue; if no private channel is available, contact the repository owner privately if possible and share only minimal non-sensitive information until a channel is agreed.

## Suspected target repositories

BeforeRun findings are evidence for further investigation, not proof of intent or a safety certification. Follow the relevant hosting provider's reporting process where appropriate. Do not submit active malware, real credentials, or target secrets to this project. A sanitized, synthetic reproduction can help discuss a skill limitation without exposing the target's sensitive content.

## Accidental secret exposure

Do not post the value. Treat it as potentially compromised: notify the affected owner through an appropriate private channel and follow their credential rotation and incident-response procedures. Redact examples and avoid sending the value to additional tools or services. Removing a public copy alone may not undo disclosure.
